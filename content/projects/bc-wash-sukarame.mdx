## Problem

The business tracked service activity manually and lacked clear insight into peak demand and resource usage, making staffing and purchasing decisions reactive.

## Solution

I created a web-based dashboard with mobile-friendly data entry, trend visualizations, and consumable tracking to support faster daily decisions.

## Tools Used

- Next.js for the web interface.
- Relational storage for transaction logging.
- Dashboard charts for demand and utilization trends.

## Impact

- Better visibility into busy-hour patterns.
- More proactive replenishment planning.
- Demonstrated transferability of warehouse analytics practices to small-business operations.

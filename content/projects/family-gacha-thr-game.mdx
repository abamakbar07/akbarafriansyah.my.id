## Problem

Manual THR distribution during family gatherings made it difficult to ensure fairness and track claimed envelopes.

## Solution

I built a simple gacha-style web experience where participants draw digitally, while an admin panel tracks claims and remaining envelopes in real time.

## Tools Used

- Next.js and lightweight client-side logic.
- Vercel deployment for quick seasonal rollout.

## Impact

- Made the process more transparent and engaging.
- Reduced confusion in envelope tracking during events.
- Showcases product thinking beyond formal workplace projects.

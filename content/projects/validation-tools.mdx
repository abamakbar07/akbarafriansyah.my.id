## Problem

Warehouse and SAP reports were reviewed manually, consuming hours and often detecting issues too late for same-day corrective action.

## Solution

I developed reusable Python and VBA tools that parse high-volume Excel reports, detect anomalies (duplicates, quantity mismatches, outliers), and generate clear issue summaries for fast follow-up.

## Tools Used

- Python with Pandas for parsing and anomaly detection.
- Excel VBA macros for teams working directly in spreadsheet workflows.
- Rule-based thresholds configurable per warehouse/project.

## Impact

- Reduced recurring report validation time by around 80%.
- Improved confidence in outbound KPI reporting.
- Helped teams focus on corrective actions rather than manual checking.
